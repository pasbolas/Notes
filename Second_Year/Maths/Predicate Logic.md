## Exam-Level Quantifier Translation Questions

Assume the **domain is all people**, unless stated otherwise.

---

### Question 1

Let

- ( S(x) ): “x is a student”
    
- ( P(x) ): “x passed the exam”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) All students passed the exam.  
b) Some students did not pass the exam.  
c) Only students passed the exam.  
d) Not all students passed the exam.

---

### Question 2

Let

- ( E(x) ): “x is enrolled in the course”
    
- ( A(x) ): “x attended the lectures”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) Every enrolled student attended the lectures.  
b) Some enrolled students did not attend the lectures.  
c) Only enrolled students attended the lectures.  
d) There is an enrolled student who attended the lectures.

---

### Question 3

Let

- ( C(x) ): “x owns a car”
    
- ( L(x) ): “x has a driving licence”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) Everyone who owns a car has a driving licence.  
b) Some people own a car but do not have a driving licence.  
c) Only people with a driving licence own a car.  
d) Not everyone who owns a car has a driving licence.

---

### Question 4

Let

- ( W(x) ): “x works part-time”
    
- ( S(x) ): “x is a student”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) All students work part-time.  
b) Some students do not work part-time.  
c) Only students work part-time.  
d) There exists a student who works part-time.

---

### Question 5

Let

- ( P(x) ): “x passed the module”
    
- ( R(x) ): “x repeated the module”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) No one who repeated the module passed it.  
b) Some people repeated the module and passed it.  
c) Only people who passed the module did not repeat it.  
d) Not all people who repeated the module failed.

---

### Question 6 (Trick-heavy, very exam-like)

Let

- ( B(x) ): “x submitted the assignment”
    
- ( M(x) ): “x received full marks”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) Everyone who received full marks submitted the assignment.  
b) Some people submitted the assignment but did not receive full marks.  
c) Only people who submitted the assignment received full marks.  
d) Not everyone who submitted the assignment received full marks.

---

### Question 7 (Negation focus)

Let

- ( T(x) ): “x attended the tutorial”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) All students attended the tutorial.  
b) The negation of “All students attended the tutorial”.  
c) Some students did not attend the tutorial.

---

### Question 8 (Careful with “only”)

Let

- ( F(x) ): “x failed the exam”
    
- ( S(x) ): “x studied”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) Only students who studied passed the exam.  
b) Some students studied but still failed the exam.  
c) Not all students who studied passed the exam.

---

### Question 9 (Mixed structure)

Let

- ( H(x) ): “x has a laptop”
    
- ( C(x) ): “x completed the project”
    

Write in logical notation:

a) Everyone who completed the project has a laptop.  
b) Some people have a laptop but did not complete the project.  
c) Only people with a laptop completed the project.

---

### Question 10 (Very typical final-exam question)

Let

- ( M(x) ): “x is a module”
    
- ( D(x) ): “x is difficult”
    

Domain: all modules.

Write in logical notation:

a) All modules are difficult.  
b) Some modules are not difficult.  
c) Not all modules are difficult.
